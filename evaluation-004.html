COMET-Based English–Arabic Translation Evaluation

A hybrid evaluation combining neural metric scores (wmt22-comet-da) with expert human assessment across three SOTA LLMs.

Project Overview

This study benchmarks ChatGPT (GPT-5.5), Gemini 3.6 Flash, and Claude Sonnet 5 Thinking on English-to-Arabic healthcare translation using COMET—a neural evaluation metric trained on human judgments that evaluates semantic preservation rather than simple n-gram overlap.

Automatic Quantitative Results (wmt22-comet-da)

COMET scores (>0.90 indicates excellent semantic fidelity to human reference translation).

Automatic vs. Human Alignment Analysis

COMET Metric Perspective

"Evaluates neural embeddings against professional human reference. Ranks Gemini (0.9146) slightly above Claude (0.9097) due to tight semantic overlap."

Expert Human Perspective

"Prefers Claude over Gemini because Claude maintains a neutral, publication-ready register, whereas Gemini introduces literary metaphors ('يرسم ملامح جديدة')."

Key Findings & Demonstration

All 3 LLMs scored >0.90 on COMET, demonstrating publication-grade quality Proved COMET & human evaluation align on top performer (ChatGPT GPT-5.5) Highlighted limitations of neural metrics in distinguishing stylistic register shifts
