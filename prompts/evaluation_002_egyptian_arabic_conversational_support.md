# Objective

Evaluate and compare the ability of ChatGPT and Google Gemini to generate natural, supportive, and culturally appropriate conversational responses in Egyptian Arabic.

The evaluation focuses on how well each model follows the prompt, produces authentic Egyptian Arabic, communicates empathy, and generates responses that resemble natural conversations between close friends.

---

## Evaluation Scope

This evaluation examines the ability of Large Language Models to generate supportive conversational responses in Egyptian Arabic.

The assessment focuses on a single conversational support task and compares model performance using a structured human evaluation framework. The evaluation emphasizes linguistic quality, instruction following, cultural appropriateness, empathy, and conversational naturalness within the context of informal Egyptian Arabic.

---

## Evaluation Workflow

This evaluation follows the workflow defined in the **LLM Evaluation Project Standard (LEPS) v1.0**.

1. Define the evaluation objective.
2. Design the conversational prompt.
3. Generate responses using the selected LLMs.
4. Evaluate each response using predefined criteria.
5. Compare model performance across all evaluation dimensions.
6. Analyze strengths and weaknesses.
7. Document findings, limitations, and conclusions.

# Prompt

## Original Prompt (Egyptian Arabic)

صاحبك بعتلك الرسالة دي:

"أنا مضغوط أوي من الشغل وحاسس إني مش قادر أكمل."

اكتب رد باللهجة المصرية:

- يكون طبيعي وكأن شخص مصري هو اللي كتبه.
- يكون داعم ومشجع.
- من 50 إلى 80 كلمة.
- بدون فصحى أو لهجات عربية أخرى.
- مناسب لإرسالها في تطبيق مراسلة.
- لا تقدم نصائح طبية أو نفسية متخصصة.

# Evaluation Criteria

The responses were evaluated using the following criteria:

- Instruction Following
- Egyptian Arabic Naturalness
- Fluency
- Dialect Consistency & Spelling
- Clarity
- Empathy
- Tone
- Cultural Appropriateness
- Overall Preference

Each criterion is scored on a **5-point scale**, where **5 represents excellent performance**.

Small score differences (for example, **4.5 vs. 5.0**) represent meaningful qualitative distinctions between two high-quality responses rather than large differences in overall capability.

---

# Evaluation Methodology

This case study compares one response generated by each model using a single prompt. Its purpose is to demonstrate a structured LLM evaluation workflow rather than establish a general ranking of overall model performance.

The evaluation was conducted by a single native speaker of Egyptian Arabic using the predefined rubric described above. The scores represent one evaluator's assessment. Future studies could incorporate multiple independent evaluators to measure inter-rater agreement and reduce subjective bias.

Both models were evaluated using the same prompt and evaluation criteria. Performance may vary across prompts, domains, and conversation contexts. Therefore, the findings should be interpreted only within the scope of this evaluation.

## Results Summary

The evaluation produced the following key findings:

- Both ChatGPT and Google Gemini fully satisfied the prompt requirements and generated fluent, supportive responses in Egyptian Arabic.
- ChatGPT demonstrated excellent instruction following, fluency, and dialect consistency.
- Google Gemini produced a more conversational and emotionally engaging response, closely reflecting everyday Egyptian communication.
- The evaluation identified meaningful qualitative differences in conversational naturalness, empathy, and tone despite both models achieving high overall performance.
- For this specific conversational support task, Google Gemini was the preferred model based on overall conversational authenticity.

# Overall Preference

## Preferred Model

**Google Gemini**

### Justification

Both models successfully fulfilled every prompt requirement and generated high-quality responses in Egyptian Arabic.

ChatGPT produced a fluent, supportive, and well-structured reply using natural Egyptian expressions. Google Gemini, however, generated a response that felt more authentic to everyday Egyptian conversation. Its conversational tone, familiar expressions, and concrete offer of companionship created a stronger emotional connection.

Although ChatGPT demonstrated slightly better dialect consistency in writing, Google Gemini more effectively captured the communication style of close Egyptian friends. For **this conversational support task**, Google Gemini produced the more natural and emotionally engaging response.

---

# Lessons Learned

This evaluation demonstrates that assessing dialectal language generation requires more than verifying prompt compliance or linguistic correctness. Authenticity, cultural familiarity, emotional connection, and conversational style are equally important when evaluating responses in Egyptian Arabic.

The comparison also shows that multiple high-quality responses can satisfy the same prompt while differing in subtle aspects of naturalness and user experience. A structured evaluation rubric helps identify these qualitative differences in a transparent and consistent manner.

Finally, this case study highlights the importance of documenting evaluation methodology, acknowledging limitations, and providing evidence-based justifications to improve the credibility and reproducibility of LLM assessments.

# Conclusion

This case study demonstrates a structured approach to evaluating large language models for Egyptian Arabic conversational support.

Both ChatGPT and Google Gemini fully satisfied the prompt requirements and produced fluent, supportive, and culturally appropriate responses. The evaluation identified relatively small but meaningful differences in conversational naturalness, empathy, tone, and dialect consistency.

For this specific conversational support task, Google Gemini produced the preferred response because it more closely reflected the communication style of everyday Egyptian conversations and offered more tangible emotional support. ChatGPT, however, demonstrated excellent instruction following, strong fluency, and highly consistent written Egyptian Arabic.

Overall, the evaluation illustrates that high-quality dialect generation depends not only on linguistic correctness but also on authenticity, empathy, and alignment with real-world conversational norms.

---

# Study Limitations

This evaluation is based on a single prompt and one response from each model. While it demonstrates a structured LLM evaluation methodology, it should not be interpreted as a general ranking of overall model performance.

The assessment was conducted by a single native speaker of Egyptian Arabic. Although a structured rubric was used, subjective criteria such as empathy, tone, and conversational naturalness may vary between evaluators. Future work could incorporate multiple independent native-speaker raters to measure inter-rater agreement and further strengthen the reliability of the evaluation.

Model performance may also vary across prompts, domains, conversation contexts, and future model updates. Consequently, the findings presented here should be interpreted only within the scope of this specific evaluation task.

## Model Selection

This evaluation compares ChatGPT (GPT-5.5) with Google Gemini 3.6 Flash. The objective is to evaluate the models that were available and selected for this task rather than to compare equivalent performance tiers. Because flagship and lightweight models may differ in capability, future studies should compare models within similar tiers to improve experimental fairness.
