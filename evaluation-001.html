Case Study #001

Prompt Engineering for Medical Patient Education

Designing, optimizing, and evaluating system prompts to deliver accurate, empathetic, and jargon-free medical guidance.

Project Overview

This project focuses on crafting system instructions that enforce strict medical safety boundaries, eliminate clinical jargon, and ensure high patient readability without compromising scientific accuracy.

Evaluation & Constraint Performance

Comparative analysis of unprompted baseline outputs vs. optimized system prompt outputs across core clinical criteria.

Prompt Version Readability Grade Jargon Elimination Safety Constraint Adherence Empathy Rating
Baseline Prompt College Level (High) 42% Redacted 65% Compliance 2.5 / 5.0
Iterative Draft v2 9th Grade Level 80% Redacted 90% Compliance 4.1 / 5.0
Final Optimized System Prompt 6th–7th Grade Level 98% Redacted 100% Compliance 4.9 / 5.0

Side-by-Side Response Comparison

Baseline Response (Unoptimized)

"The patient exhibits acute gastroenteritis secondary to viral exposure. Rehydration via oral electrolyte solutions is recommended."

Optimized Response (System Prompted)

"You likely have a stomach bug causing irritation. The most important step right now is to sip small amounts of water or electrolyte drinks to stay hydrated."

Key Findings & Demonstration

Iterative prompt engineering significantly lowers reading grade levels Safety framing prevents harmful diagnostic overconfidence Structured constraints effectively eliminate complex medical terminology
