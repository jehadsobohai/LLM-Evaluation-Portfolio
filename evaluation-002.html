Egyptian Arabic Conversational Support Evaluation

Evaluating ChatGPT (GPT-5.5) and Google Gemini (3.6 Flash) for natural, supportive, and culturally authentic Egyptian Arabic conversational responses.

Project Overview

This evaluation benchmarks LLMs on their ability to act as a close friend sending a supportive message in Egyptian Dialectal Arabic. Evaluated under the LEPS v1.0 framework by a native speaker across 8 qualitative criteria.

Model Response Comparison

ChatGPT (GPT-5.5)

"بص يا صاحبي، أنا عارف إن الضغط لما بيزيد بيخلي الواحد يحس إنه خلاص مش قادر... ولو حبيت تفضفض..."

Evaluation: Excellent dialect consistency and spelling, but slightly more polished than casual chat between friends.

Google Gemini (3.6 Flash) — Preferred 🥇

"ولا يهمك يا صاحبي، الشغل عمره ما بيخلص... وتعال ننزل نشرب كباية شاي أو قهوة... الشغل يروح في داهية."

Evaluation: Highly authentic phrases ("يروح في داهية") and offers concrete companionship (tea/coffee), driving higher empathy.

Key Findings & Demonstration

Google Gemini 3.6 Flash selected as preferred model for dialectal authenticity ChatGPT GPT-5.5 demonstrated superior written dialect consistency Structured rubrics isolate subtle tone and cultural nuances from basic fluency
