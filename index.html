About Me

I am a Medical Doctor with a strong interest in Large Language Model evaluation, Arabic Natural Language Processing (NLP), prompt engineering, and AI quality assurance.

This portfolio showcases practical evaluation projects covering translation quality, conversational AI, educational content, and multilingual language models. Each project demonstrates structured evaluation methodologies that combine expert human assessment with automated evaluation techniques to produce transparent, reproducible, and evidence-based results.

My current work focuses on developing practical evaluation workflows, improving evaluation quality, and expanding this portfolio with projects in medical AI, reasoning evaluation, hallucination detection, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI safety, multilingual benchmarking, and evaluation automation.
