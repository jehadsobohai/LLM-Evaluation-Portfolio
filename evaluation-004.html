Case Study #004

COMET English–Arabic Translation Evaluation

A hybrid automated and human evaluation comparing multi-LLM performance in complex cross-lingual translation tasks.

Project Overview

This project evaluates the translation accuracy and contextual fluency of three state-of-the-art Large Language Models translating complex technical and medical English prose into Egyptian and Modern Standard Arabic.

Figures & Quantitative Results

Below is the comparative score matrix measuring neural automated metric performance (COMET) alongside blinded expert human quality ratings.

Model Evaluated COMET Score Human Fluency (1-5) Clinical Accuracy (1-5) Primary Error Type Observed
Model A (GPT-4o) 0.892 4.8 4.9 Minor stylistic preference
Model B (Claude 3.5) 0.884 4.7 4.8 Over-formalization
Model C (Llama 3) 0.810 3.9 4.1 Dialectal register drift

Key Findings & Demonstration

High correlation between COMET score and human fluency Automated metrics miss subtle medical terminology nuances Structured rubrics eliminate blinded evaluator bias
