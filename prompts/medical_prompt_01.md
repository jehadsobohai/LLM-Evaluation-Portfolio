# Medical Prompt 01 – Patient Education

## Objective

Create an AI prompt that explains hypertension to a newly diagnosed patient using simple, non-medical language.

---

## Initial Prompt

Explain hypertension.

---

## Problems

- Too technical
- No target audience
- Unstructured response
- Medical terminology may confuse patients

---

## Improved Prompt

You are an experienced family physician.

Explain hypertension to a patient who has just been diagnosed.

Requirements:
- Use simple English.
- Keep the response under 200 words.
- Explain:
  - What hypertension is
  - Common causes
  - Symptoms
  - Treatment
  - Lifestyle changes
- Avoid medical jargon.
- End with three key takeaways.

---

## Expected Improvements

- Easier for patients to understand
- Better structure
- More complete information
- Appropriate for non-medical readers
