Case Study #003

HITL Human-in-the-Loop Translation Evaluation

Evidence-based qualitative error taxonomy and human scoring protocol for English to Arabic translation quality assurance.

Project Overview

Automated metrics often fail to capture semantic nuances in translation. This project establishes a human-in-the-loop (HITL) qualitative error taxonomy to score accuracy, fluency, and terminology fidelity.

Linguistic Error Categorization

Breakdown of qualitative human evaluation across evaluated translation sets.

Error Category Definition Severity Impact Frequency Count
Critical Mistranslation Alters underlying clinical or legal meaning High (Disqualifying) 2 Observed
Terminology Inconsistency Uses non-standard technical vocabulary Medium 6 Observed
Grammatical Agreement Gender or number disagreement in Arabic syntax Low 11 Observed

Blinded Evaluation Sample

Source Text

"The clinical trial demonstrated statistically significant reduction in symptom severity."

Evaluator Critique

"أظهرت التجربة السريرية انخفاضًا ملحوظًا إحصائيًا في شدة الأعراض."

Human Feedback: Correctly translates technical phrases ("statistically significant" → "ملحوظًا إحصائيًا"). Grade: Pass.

Key Findings & Demonstration

Designed structured qualitative error taxonomy Implemented blinded human scoring protocols Delivered actionable human feedback loops for model fine-tuning
