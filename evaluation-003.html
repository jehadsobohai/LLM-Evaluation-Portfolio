Human-in-the-Loop English–Arabic Translation

Evaluating ChatGPT (GPT-5.5), Google Gemini (3.6 Flash), and Claude (Sonnet 5 Thinking) on healthcare translation quality.

Project Overview

This case study evaluates healthcare machine translation using an initial AI-generated evaluation refined by an independent Human Linguistic Analysis. The final rankings prioritize stylistic fidelity, medical terminology, and Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) conventions.

Linguistic Analysis & Style Comparisons

ChatGPT (GPT-5.5) — Ranked 1st 🥇

"يُحدث الذكاء الاصطناعي تحولًا في قطاع الرعاية الصحية... ينبغي أن يكون أداةً مكملةً لعمل المتخصصين..."

Strength: Closest match to source neutral professional register without interpretive embellishment.

Google Gemini (3.6 Flash) — Ranked 3rd 🥉

"يقوم الذكاء الاصطناعي برسم ملامح جديدة للرعاية الصحية... إمكانات هائلة..."

Weakness: Stylistically elevated ("يرسم ملامح جديدة") and overly emphatic ("هائلة") compared to neutral English text.
